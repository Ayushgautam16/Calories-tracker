# 🍎 Calories Tracker App

**Calories Tracker** is a simple and efficient tool to help users monitor their daily calorie intake, track their meals, and maintain a healthy lifestyle. Whether you're trying to lose weight, gain muscle, or simply eat healthier, this app gives you the data and motivation you need.

---

## 🧠 Features

- 🍽️ **Add Meals** – Log food items with calorie counts.
- 📊 **Daily Summary** – View total calories consumed per day.
- 🕒 **Meal Timing** – Categorize meals into Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.
- 🧮 **Calorie Calculator** – Estimate daily calorie needs based on age, weight, height, and activity level.
- 📆 **History Tracking** – View and compare daily calorie logs.
- 🎯 **Set Goals** – Define your daily calorie target and monitor progress.
- 📱 **Responsive UI** – Mobile and desktop friendly (if web-based).
